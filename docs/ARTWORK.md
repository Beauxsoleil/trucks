# Artwork — stage 2

Created with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool for this project, inspired by the requested simple cartoon game-art direction. These are original generated assets, not downloads from Kenney and not claimed to have Kenney's CC0 license.

Files: `public/assets/monster-truck.png`, `public/assets/dirt-track.png`, `public/assets/wooden-blocks.png`.

Truck and blocks retain genuine PNG alpha. The flatter edit variants introduced a baked-in checkerboard, so those variants were rejected. The selected art retains some shading; it is concept art rather than strictly flat vector artwork.

## Generation prompts

### Truck

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a single game asset PNG for a toddler's monster-truck game: a friendly orange-and-blue monster truck in 3/4 side view facing right, big smiling expressive headlights, cheerful curved grille smile, very thick oversized tires. Flat cartoon game art similar to Kenney.nl: bold dark navy outlines, bright solid flat colors, simple chunky shapes, no gradients, no shading, no fine detail, no scary or aggressive styling. Entire truck visible with a little padding, centered and filling the image. Truly transparent background, no ground, no scene, no text, no logos, no watermark.

### Dirt track

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a wide landscape background PNG for a toddler monster-truck game, aspect ratio 3:2: a broad simple warm ochre dirt track across foreground, gentle soft rolling green hills across the middle and clear light blue sky with two simple rounded white clouds. Flat cartoon game art similar to Kenney.nl, bold navy outlines, bright flat solid colors, chunky soft shapes. Low detail quiet composition for UI overlays, no objects in foreground. No gradients, no fine detail, no vehicles, no blocks, no people, no text, no logos, no watermark.

### Blocks

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a single game asset PNG for a toddler monster-truck game: exactly THREE colorful wooden toy cube blocks stacked vertically, red block on top, blue block in middle, yellow block at bottom. Slight 3/4 perspective, simple flat colored faces and bold dark navy outlines, gently rounded corners. Cartoon game art similar to Kenney.nl, chunky friendly shapes, bright flat colors, no gradients, no grain or fine detail. Entire stack visible with a little padding, centered, truly transparent background, no ground, no other objects, no letters or numbers, no text or watermark.

## Background refinement

Preserve the subject, composition and colors exactly; simplify surfaces to uniform flat color regions. Remove gradients, glow, airbrush effects, highlights and texture. Thick navy outlines and simple toddler cartoon art. Keep sky, hills, and dirt track; opaque background.
